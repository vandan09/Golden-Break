Golden Break – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Golden Break ("the app") is a block puzzle game published by Dizzo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (score, coins, ceramic progress, gallery, settings, streaks) is saved locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and there is no account or sign-in.

Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob shows ads. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them where you consent. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) collects anonymous usage data such as sessions, game events and device type so we can understand how the game is used and improve it.
  • Google Play services provide the in-app review prompt and, if you allow it, notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Consent and your choices

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and UK), the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are served. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings where offered. You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send a daily reminder. You can turn this off in your device settings at any time.

Children

Golden Break is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Data held by the third-party services above is retained according to their policies. Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
